The accompanying COPYING, COPYING.NONGPL and COPYING.README files are copied
unchanged from the official v3 tag and published beside the archive on the VPS.
See those notices for the GPL-2.0-or-later licence and listed asset exceptions.
The manifest records provenance; the app pins the archive URL, size and hash
instead of trusting a mutable remote manifest to choose new engine content.
