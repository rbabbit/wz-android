# Warzone Towers 0.19 — 46-map browser and defence lines

Version 0.19 adds **43 new Towers maps**: 15 Easy, 15 Medium and 13 Hard.
Greenhaven joins Easy, while the original Dustfall and Iron Valley maps join
Hard. The full catalog therefore contains **46 maps: 16 Easy, 15 Medium and
15 Hard**. The difficulty labels are authoring targets; they are not playtest
results.

| Group | New maps | Existing maps | Total | New-map rounds / preparation |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Easy | 15 | Greenhaven | 16 | 15 rounds / 240 seconds |
| Medium | 15 | — | 15 | 20 rounds / 180 seconds |
| Hard | 13 | Dustfall, Iron Valley | 15 | 25 rounds / 150 seconds |

Greenhaven retains its 15-round schedule. Dustfall and Iron Valley retain
their original 30-round schedules. A satellite uplink is added to Greenhaven's
starting base; the existing Greenhaven structures remain in place.

The native start screen now reads its map metadata from a shared catalog.
Easy, Medium and Hard tabs show their counts, and swipeable cards show each
map's name, short description, round count and position in the selected group.
The preview PNGs are schematic renderings of the actual native map data;
they are not gameplay screenshots or 3D renders. Selection is retained across
the Graphics and Music paths and home returns. The original splash artwork,
graphics/music/HQ options and the separate Cannon/Rockets Duel choices remain.

Defence drag placement previews up to 64 candidate sites per line; native
validation determines which sites can be accepted.
Its runtime gesture handling, native orders and completed construction remain
unverified for this revision.

The authored map/scenario files and supported package append/reindex changes
are generated by `tools/generate-levels-019.py` and
`tools/build-levels-019.py`. The latter's asset integrity report is
[archive/levels-0.19-package.json](../../archive/levels-0.19-package.json).
It records preservation of the complete 0.18 data prefix, the normalized
compiled engine body and WASM. These are source/package integrity findings,
not evidence of native gameplay.

Verification is restricted to source audit/review, an Android build and a
brief native home-screen emulator check. On the exact delivery APK, the Easy,
Medium and Hard tabs and real previews appeared; swiping selected Fernpass
and updated its Play label. The 1080x600 normal layout and 854x480 layout at
1.3 font scale fit on screen. The original emulator profile was restored.
The hash-bound record and screenshots are in `qa/0.19-home/report.json`.
**Gameplay, pathfinding, balance, complete waves and the defence drag gesture
are unverified.** No gameplay run is authorized
for this revision. Older QA reports and screenshots remain historical and do
not verify the new maps, home screen or gesture.

The complete 0.18 APK, source ZIP and checksum file remain frozen at
`C:/timerApp/`, with their exact paths, sizes and hashes recorded in
[archive/fallback-0.19.json](../../archive/fallback-0.19.json). The source ZIP
is referenced without making another fallback copy. GitHub `main` and
`v0.17-benchmark` remain the accepted benchmark. Older tags/artifacts and the
separate v6.4 fallback remain preserved.

The Android package remains `com.rbabbit.warzonetowers.lab`. Intended delivery
names are `Warzone-Towers-0.19.apk`, `warzone-towers-0.19-source.zip` and
`Warzone-Towers-0.19-SHA256.txt`. Their existence or integrity is not established
by these notes; delivery packaging follows the final recorded build and
