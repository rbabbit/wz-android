# Warzone Towers 0.26 — portable rewards and cleared-wave coins

Purchased rewards now belong to a shared on-phone inventory and can be used on
any map. Choose quantities to bring into the next match rather than binding a
purchase to the map currently displayed. Bulk purchase controls support 1, 5,
10, 15 and 20, with an additional +5 adjustment. The expanded catalog focuses
on fortresses and artillery; AA, repair structures and trucks are not sold.

The selected match loadout permits at most three fortresses in total across
fortress types. Reward structures still require their normal in-game power to
build. Unused building items return to inventory after the run; research and
supply boosts are consumed when the match starts. The previous wallet balance
is retained, and unspent map-bound purchases migrate into global inventory.
Updating does not issue another starter gift.

The catalog contains 19 choices: four fortresses, six advanced direct-fire
defences, five artillery structures, a Wide Spectrum Sensor Tower, a Laser
Satellite Command Post, research and supply. Cannon/Missile/Mass Driver/Heavy
Rocket fortress prices are 120/180/200/150 crystals. Other defence/artillery
items cost 10–30, the sensor 10 and the satellite post 250; research/supply
retain their 60/30 prices. The canonical entries are in `RewardCatalog.java`.

For a Laser Satellite Command Post, select the owned completed post, choose
FIRE SAT and tap a visible enemy unit or structure. Back or CANCEL SAT cancels
targeting. Native readiness, cooldown and enemy-target validation remain in
charge of firing.

Each actually cleared wave earns five coins once in that run. These rewards
use the native wave's tracked enemy IDs rather than the displayed wave number
or a countdown. The old ten-wave milestone payout is removed. Victory bonuses
remain 60/80/100 for Easy/Medium/Hard, plus 50 for first completion of a map.
The terminal receipt separates wave earnings from the victory bonus and shows
amounts already credited, without paying them twice. Quitting does not create
an extra outcome bonus.

Both outcomes retain the same-map Home return from 0.25. The existing compact
native rewards/receipt layout is extended; the accepted references remain in
`docs/ui-0.25/`, with no new mockups or current emulator screenshots claimed.

Verification is limited to source review, focused source-only fixtures, Android
compilation and package/archive integrity. Emulator testing is not authorized.
Current screens, Android persistence, construction, targeting and gameplay are
not claimed runtime verified. Synthetic checks retain their own provenance.

The complete 0.25 APK/source/checksums remain frozen in the fallback record
`archive/fallback-0.26.json`. Native reward and wave-observation hooks are
appended and reindexed without modifying the compiled engine body/WASM,
original data prefix, map payloads or unrelated virtual files. The authored
enemy roster and wave timing, music, Duel and older benchmarks remain preserved.
No VPS or GitHub benchmark files are changed.

The package remains `com.rbabbit.warzonetowers.lab`, labelled **Warzone Towers**,
with version code 26 / version 0.26. Delivery artifacts are
`Warzone-Towers-0.26.apk`, `warzone-towers-0.26-source.zip` and
`Warzone-Towers-0.26-SHA256.txt`. The source ZIP retains complete native and map
sources, reward implementation and deferred Duel code.
