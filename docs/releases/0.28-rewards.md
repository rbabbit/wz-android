# Warzone Towers 0.28 — compact illustrated Rewards

Home and in-game Rewards follow the selected compact drawer: real bundled
game thumbnails, direct Fortresses / Defences / Artillery / Support controls,
short item rows, one selected purchase and compact quantities. The stock
category popup is removed. Existing catalog prices, native construction power
costs and three-fortress policy remain unchanged.

The focused purchase check exposed a quota-ordering bug: after buying five
more items, an older native wave packet could overwrite the phone's larger
allowance. A loaded older save could also report inventory before purchase
reconciliation completed. Purchase journal ordering now protects the phone
allowance, and restored inventory is accepted only after the native resume
handshake. The wallet's current quantities and crystal charges remain tied
to the same economic run. The actual ledger/store implementation has focused
JVM checks; these remain separate from the final emulator observations.

The exact final APK passed ten focused emulator checks: Home and in-game
purchases, a stable bulk allowance of six items on both the native and phone
ledgers, one normal paid construction consuming one item, manual native saves,
older/newer save loads retaining five remaining items, and an app restart/load
retaining balance50 and those five items. Native and phone quantities were
observed, rather than inferred from the UI alone.

The reported phone screenshot shows a native autosave `writeGameFile` failure
and lacks the SAVE header entry that shipped in 0.27. That is evidence of a UI
or build mismatch, not proof of the installed package's version. This pass
checks build identity and the exact new APK's live purchase, native saving,
private phone archive and Saved Games load. The specific phone installation
cannot be certified without observing that device.

An earlier 0.28 candidate using the identical frozen native payload also
recorded a genuine native automatic save. That observation is retained as
supplemental evidence for the native writer; the final candidate's manual
save, live quantities and resume behavior must be checked separately after
the wallet-ordering fix.

The known 0.27 Greenhaven internal level alias correction remains intact:
`Greenhaven-T1` fits the native saved-game header's UTF-8 length below20 bytes.
All 46 names remain within that limit. The native engine JS/data/WASM and all
map, wave, music and deferred Duel bytes remain identical to 0.27.

Verification is deliberately bounded: source/build/signature/archive checks
and a small actual emulator journey recorded against the delivery APK hash.
Source/JVM fixtures do not establish Android gameplay; screenshots alone do
not establish native saving or buying. Full gameplay, every map, a complete
campaign and physical-device rendering/performance are outside this pass.
The emulator's previously documented black-terrain limitation remains.

The complete 0.27 APK/source/checksums are preserved in
`archive/fallback-0.28.json`. Intended outputs are
`Warzone-Towers-0.28.apk`, `warzone-towers-0.28-source.zip` and
`Warzone-Towers-0.28-SHA256.txt`. The source archive retains the complete
native and map sources, signed-package evidence and the deferred Duel code.
VPS files, GitHub benchmarks, older artifacts and v6.4 remain untouched.
