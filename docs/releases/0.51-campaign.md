# v0.51 — Campaign alongside Towers

Home has a separate **CAMPAIGN** button beside Play. Towers retains its existing
maps, waves, rewards, saves and controls. Campaign starts the original Alpha
story and uses the original mission objectives and chapter progression.

Download the campaign movies once from the owner's existing VPS, then choose
**NEW CAMPAIGN**. **CONTINUE** lists campaign games saved on this phone through
the in-game SAVE button. Campaign saves and Towers saves use separate stores.

The download is the pinned 215,283,360-byte pack, SHA-256
`23f74895fa7f03cb86a79b857bad6977ce18d20b8a944d51449e68517e80bc47`.
Only its 179 movies plus COPYING/VERSION are installed. The older engine files
inside the archive are never installed. Interrupted downloads can resume;
installed movies are verified before becoming available offline.

The Campaign header includes **ALL COMBAT**. Its selection intent is separate
from construction-truck selection and pauses for native briefings, movies and
transport controls. Turn it off for individual unit control. Research remains
manual, including the original campaign's artefact requirements.

The current native engine is rebuilt from the preserved source with narrow
Campaign gates for native UI observations, input, selection and the opening
movie. Campaign does not enable Towers' forced-autosave suppression. Original
mission bodies are retained, with namespaced mobile hooks appended. See
`archive/native-campaign-0.51/` and the matching build/integration tools.

The immutable v0.50 APK and complete source remain at their original paths and
are pinned by `archive/fallback-0.51.json`. GitHub and VPS content are unchanged.

Runtime coverage and remaining limits are recorded with the exact delivery APK
in `archive/campaign-0.51-emulator-checks.json`. Host-only save/media fixtures do
not establish actual campaign gameplay or audiovisual playback. A complete
campaign playthrough and physical-phone performance are not claimed.

The delivery APK passed the opening Alpha journey: offline opening movie,
original guide, repeated combat movement, ordinary Command Centre/factory
construction, native truck production, waiting-order cancellation, menu and
Intelligence returns, minimap/finger camera control, and phone Save/Continue.
The original Towers route was checked for visible-truck selection, menu returns
and native camera movement. Campaign setup and menus were also inspected at a
1280 × 720 physical profile (1063 × 598 effective CSS viewport under the existing
engine scaling). No research topic after artefact recovery, later mission,
off-world transport or chapter transition was played through.

The test runner reused the prepared native world after correcting two fixture
waits: it no longer sends Back twice against a cached paused packet, and it waits
for the command bridge's first snapshot separately from save hydration. The
emulator's existing black-terrain appearance also affects v0.50; visible models,
native coordinates/orders and map geometry were checked. This release does not
claim a terrain-rendering or audible-playback verification.
