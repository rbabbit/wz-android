# v0.54 — Campaign Intelligence, build overlays and footer

The exact installed APK passed sixteen focused emulator cases. Its SHA-256 is
`89c842519c2c34cdb87fec002d98a2d0bf6277a2058feaccdd9f7d731c795f99`.
The complete v0.53 APK and source ZIP remain preserved in
`archive/fallback-0.54.json`.

Campaign's unit-drawer Intelligence opens the existing dark research-report
reader over the battlefield. It reads completed research and report availability
from the original engine rather than inventing reports. Original mission
briefings remain available through a separate handoff to native Intelligence.
The handoff code waits for the model preview to retire before Research, Build,
Save or a native briefing receives input. Native videos, transport panels and mission/load
changes keep ownership of their original UI and invalidate obsolete requests.
The Campaign handoff also prevents one physical header tap from activating both
its touch-release handler and a replayed click, which could open and immediately
close the destination page.

Campaign construction models could be present in the renderer's accepted-site
state while invisible on screen: Emscripten's native soft-fullscreen startup had
set the sibling overlay canvas to `display:none`. The Campaign-only renderer
guard restores that canvas while the world view is visible, and hides it when
an app/native panel owns the screen. It preserves native placement validation,
construction orders, accepted plans and the camera/input implementation.

The white **− / SHOW** collapse button is removed from both Campaign and Towers.
Their Analysis, Manufacturing and Build controls remain expanded even for users
whose saved collapse preference was true. The old preference is neither read
nor overwritten in those modes. Duel retains its actual collapse button and
its existing preference. The same activity/Build click handlers are preserved;
removing the flex child also removes its column and extra gap without changing
the six-button unit drawer.

No native engine files, loaders, data or VFS metadata are changed in this
revision. Original Campaign scripts/mobile bridge, all Towers/Duel scenarios,
maps and music remain frozen to v0.53. The packaging gate compares whole engine
files and every other unapproved app input with the preserved baseline.

Supporting host checks are separate from Android gameplay:

- `archive/footer-collapse-js-0.54-checks.json` executes the actual activity
  controller with old collapsed preferences, activity/Build actions, unavailable
  storage and unchanged Duel behavior. It also compares preserved handlers with
  the delivered v0.53 source.
- `archive/campaign-0.54/intelligence-js-checks.json` checks actual report-reader
  and Campaign HUD logic with controlled native replies, preview retirement,
  mission resets and native UI ownership.

The exact-APK journey showed the Campaign footer expanded with the old
saved collapse preference still true, two accepted building models visible,
retained models after switching the selected structure, and the HTML Intelligence
reader over an unpaused native battlefield. The original native Research Facility
at 18,50 completed normally. ALL COMBAT stayed suspended while the reader was
open. Android Back, reopening, single-tap Research/Manufacturing handoffs, explicit
native mission briefings and return to world input passed. Saving from the reader
closed it and produced a fresh phone-save acknowledgment; Continue restored the
matching archive slot and reopened the reader in the native world.

The physical 1280×720 emulator profile at density override 320 showed all three
footer controls and the Intelligence panel inside their viewport bounds. The
Towers regression verified the expanded footer with its old collapsed preference,
its existing Intelligence behavior without Campaign briefing controls, and the
Build toggle/selected-truck return. The final run recorded no runtime exceptions.

The run reuses a raw native Campaign checkpoint containing previously accepted
construction orders. No units, power, technology or finished buildings were
granted to the fixture. The UI preference `lab-ops-collapsed=true` was deliberately
set to verify that existing users keep their three footer controls accessible.
The six-button unit drawer was not changed.

The opening Alpha fixture has no report-bearing completed research in its
Intelligence history. It verifies the reader and native briefing/input handoffs;
populated report content, model animation and report audio playback are not
claimed from that empty history. Native report eligibility is unchanged.

An existing Campaign cancellation-journal delay remains: cancellation removes
the app job, while its construction-site/blueprint entry can persist until the
builders become idle and the journal reconciles. The bounded check eventually
observed it clear. v0.54 does not change that backend or promise immediate
cancellation cleanup.

The sixteen passed case records and screenshots are retained in
`archive/campaign-0.54`, with source/evidence identities bound by
`archive/campaign-0.54-emulator-checks.json` to the exact APK above.
Later missions, full Campaign playthroughs, audible playback and physical-phone
performance are not claimed. The emulator's existing dark/black terrain remains
a separate limitation. Duel's retained collapse behavior has controller coverage;
no Duel gameplay journey was run for this revision.

The recorder and packager verify and preserve the observed delivery. They do not
run gameplay, rebuild engines or publish a release.
