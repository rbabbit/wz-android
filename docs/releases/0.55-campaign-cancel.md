# v0.55 — Campaign cancellation cleanup

The exact installed APK passed ten focused native emulator cases. Its SHA-256 is
`c2f65e8c0a043b435e63ef3dd098ee13b97af3cfe4ccbd9c9c76607881a3e26e`.
The delivered v0.54 APK and complete source ZIP remain the immutable fallback;
their identities are recorded in `archive/fallback-0.55.json`.

The remaining Campaign cancellation issue was in the mobile construction journal:
an accepted cancellation removed its app job, but the accepted-site/blueprint
record could remain visible until its construction trucks became idle. v0.55
changes only the terminal cancellation path: when a job becomes cancelled, its
exact matching journal entry is retired too. It does not delete the real native
foundation. Done/blocked jobs, other workers and unrelated journal records keep
their previous behavior.

The Campaign source change is limited to `campaign/commands.js`. The
updated additive bridge is appended to the existing Campaign data, replacing
one VFS index row. The full earlier data prefix, original mission bodies and
includes, mobile events and research bridge are retained. The generated loader
program body and every other VFS row/file remain unchanged.

This revision does not rebuild the native engine or alter WASM. Apart from its
version and the Campaign loader/data package metadata, all app controls,
Intelligence, rendering, touch input, Towers/Duel scenarios, maps, music and other
assets remain frozen to v0.54. The complete-source packaging gate retains every
previous source entry and verifies the narrow changed-file inventory.

Host/controller evidence and the final installed-APK journey remain separate.
`archive/campaign-cancel-0.55-baseline.json` reproduces the old journal delay;
`archive/campaign-cancel-0.55-checks.json` records seven controlled backend groups
covering queued/active cancellation, other workers and FIFO entries, saved-state
reload, reuse of the same coordinates, done/blocked entries and Stop overrides.
`archive/campaign-0.55-payload.json` pins the actual source assembly and frozen
loader program/data prefix. These probes do not establish Android gameplay.

The ten-case native journey reused the unchanged pending-build checkpoint and
its three original trucks. Cancelling the queued Power Generator removed the
same target ID from app jobs, the construction journal and accepted models in
3,800 native milliseconds while the Research Facility job and its builders were
still active. The other worker assignment and menu returns remained correct,
and that lab then completed normally.

Cancelling active construction retired its plan while preserving native
foundation **162705**. Phone Save/Continue kept that same unfinished foundation
without resurrecting either cancelled plan. A new actual Build action resumed
and completed foundation 162705; only one Research Facility occupied that site.
Campaign Intelligence/footer and Towers Build/footer regressions also passed.
The final run recorded no runtime exceptions.

No units, power, research or structures were granted. One test wait was corrected
after a completed native facility appeared one poll before its finished-build
journal entry. The fixture then waited for both observations and continued from
the prepared world on the same APK; no product change or rebuild was made for
that polling correction. Passed native case data and screenshots are retained in
`archive/campaign-0.55` and bound to the APK/source by
`archive/campaign-0.55-emulator-checks.json`.

Later missions, full Campaign playthroughs, physical-phone performance and audible
playback are not claimed. The emulator's existing dark/black terrain remains a
separate limitation. The recorder and packager do not execute gameplay or publish
a release.
