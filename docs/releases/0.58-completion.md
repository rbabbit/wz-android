# v0.58 — Map completion and difficulty rewards

The exact installed APK passed five focused native/UI cases on emulator5558.
Its SHA-256 is
`0bc1d0dcf86d5f4c046edd617e2c08bdf1274c9f8377ded1c63e0f7dfe671d89`.
The delivered v0.57 APK and complete source ZIP remain the immutable fallback,
recorded in `archive/fallback-0.58.json`.

Towers map previews now show a compact star/Complete marker for maps in the
phone wallet's existing victory history. The map-browser Back control uses the
same utility-button face as its neighbours. Winning every actual catalogue map
in a difficulty grants 1,000 crystals once for that group: 16 Easy, 15 Medium
and 15 Hard maps. Older wallets that already completed a whole group are
reconciled once on Home; replaying a map does not earn that group bonus again.

Balance, map completion and claimed group bonuses persist together in the
existing atomic wallet. Schema 4 retains prior win history and wave/map/ad
accounting. Failed commits roll back the related changes together. A loss or
ordinary abandonment does not mark an unwon map complete.

Product changes are limited to GraphicsSetupView, MapPreviewPager, MainActivity,
RewardLedger and RewardStore, plus version. The minimal splash, Campaign, all
web/native engine assets, mission/scenario sources, maps, ad implementation,
soundtrack and save formats outside the wallet migration stay frozen to v0.57.
There is no engine build, VFS append or payload embedding. Packaging is gated on
preserving every earlier source ZIP entry and matching the signing identity.

Host and Android evidence are separate. `archive/completion-0.58-checks.json`
executes the actual RewardStore and RewardLedger on the JVM: 390 cases, including
241 existing wallet/ad cases and 149 completion cases. This covers migration,
all three catalogue groups, rollback, old paid saves, genuine loss accounting
and direct duplicate/replayed terminal callbacks. Android preference I/O is
simulated in that host fixture; these checks do not establish native gameplay.

The five exact-APK cases verify Back/touch bounds and initially absent markers;
seeded completed/uncompleted badges across the difficulty tabs; a real native
terminal win and group credit; restart/menu persistence without another credit;
and an actual unwon map followed by normal Return abandonment. User-phone
800×360dp controls and compact 640×360dp enlarged text were checked. At font scale
1.3, the Complete badge remained inside its map card without overlapping the
map position or other visible controls.

The native victory is an explicitly seeded boundary test, not a 15-wave
playthrough. The stopped-app wallet was given 15 Easy completion IDs while
Greenhaven remained incomplete and unclaimed; balance, inventory and other
wallet data were retained. An emulator-only native checkpoint kept one existing
enemy at one health within an existing guard tower's range, removed the other
hostiles/spawn backlog and advanced the saved config/wave state to its actual
end. The unchanged native tower killed that same enemy; the captured native
destruction event and wave-15 victory matched its ID and session. The wallet
rose from 380 to 1,495: 1,115 earned, including the one-time 1,000 group bonus and
existing wave/map awards. Greenhaven then showed Complete. Reopening did not
pay again. Direct callback replay and genuine defeat remain host-test coverage;
no additional fabricated Android/HTML victory or second loss fixture is claimed.

Three fixture corrections are retained in the report and raw evidence. The
first attempt already paid the correct 1,115 but its observer watched the wrong
output boundary. The single native retry observed the public native-log polling
handoff before forwarding it unchanged. Its test checkpoint omitted exactly two
saved bridge timers recreated by initialization, avoiding duplicate-timer
assertions from the earlier fixture; the original game-over polling remained.
A short map swipe was then replaced with a full observed browser swipe. Finally,
ADB shell capture had added CRLF line endings to the wallet backup: restoration
normalized only CRLF to LF and verified raw Android bytes with `exec-out`.
No product edits or candidate rebuilds were made for these fixture corrections.
Earlier observer/timer errors remain disclosed separately; zero errors is
claimed for the final passing observations, not the initial attempt.

The original wallet backup is untouched. After raw-byte verification and a cold
schema-4 migration, the emulator wallet again held balance 380, no completed
maps and no claimed difficulty bonuses, with its original inventory restored.
Private wallet XML and full native save fixtures are excluded from curated
source evidence; the report retains their hash provenance and fixture details.
