# v0.60 — EMAG Free for All

The minimal Home screen now offers Campaign, Towers and FFA. FFA uses the exact
supplied EMAG_FFA map: one human and three independent native NullBots. Choose
Easy, Medium or Hard for all three opponents. Everyone fights everyone.

The authored four-start map is preserved, including its starting constructors,
research, economy, Command Centres and satellites. Its bases deliberately have
no factories: the human and bots build them during play. Original stock ground
research, manufacturing, combat and skirmish victory rules remain authoritative.

VTOL aircraft, factories and pads are disabled. Air-only topics/templates are
gated, with the two ground research dependencies on air branches replaced by
their existing ground ancestors. Ordinary ground technology, costs and effects
are preserved. FFA has no Towers waves or crystal purchases.

The current Research, Manufacturing, Analysis, Build, Queue, Design, Intelligence,
Help and touch controls are reused. SAVE archives the full native FFA match on
the phone; FFA SAVES continues it. FFA, Campaign and Towers have separate save
directories and identity checks. Return to Main Menu reopens the FFA setup.

The original map archive and extracted content are retained under
`vendor/ffa-map/` and `map/ffa-emag/`. Rebuild the isolated runtime with
`tools/build-ffa-package.py`; check its map, policy and old-payload preservation
with `tools/check-ffa-package-060.py`. Existing native engine pairs/data, music,
icon, rewards, previous maps and earlier releases remain preserved. No GitHub,
VPS or GIF changes were made.

Verification details and limits are recorded with the exact delivery APK in
`archive/ffa-0.60-emulator-checks.json`. Focused tests cover the mode's startup,
native players/AI, controls and persistence; they do not establish full-match
balance or phone rendering/performance. The emulator's existing dark-terrain
rendering limitation remains separate from this feature.
