# v0.61 — Construction queues and camera rotation

Construction now validates spacing between queued buildings as well as existing
structures in Towers, Campaign and FFA. Normal base buildings require the same
clear tile as native placement; walls and defences retain their native packing
rules. Both app jobs and original native queue journals reserve their sites.
The bridge checks the site again before issuing an order.

An invalid plan from an older save keeps its identity and assigned queue. Its
compact status explains the obstruction instead of remaining misleadingly
Queued. Retry uses a fresh native selection of construction trucks. Cancel
removes that order and releases the subsequent work. Neither action silently
recruits distant trucks or reorders the same workers' queue. Campaign now uses
the same explicit selection for Retry. Late replies after Stop, leaving a game
or changing sessions cannot announce a new assignment.

Twist two fingers to turn the camera in either direction, including reversing
the view. Moving both fingers up/down still tilts; pinching still zooms.
Rotation releases on lift, cancellation, menus and a third finger. Returning to
one finger does not accidentally move or tap the battlefield. Rotating also
invalidates the cached visible-truck selection. Accepted one-finger pan
functions remain byte-for-byte identical to v0.60.

Delivered v0.60 APK and complete source are pinned in
`archive/fallback-0.61.json`. Only reviewed construction bridge entries change
inside the three native payloads; compiled engine bodies, WASM, original
Campaign missions and hidden Duel payload remain preserved. The icon, maps,
art, music, ads and reward rules are unchanged.

Verification records are `archive/construction-0.61-payload.json`,
`archive/construction-0.61-emulator-checks.json` and
`archive/construction-clean-apk-0.61.json`. Host fixtures exercise spacing,
queue/recovery and gesture transitions. Emulator evidence uses ordinary native
checkpoints and starter units, including the reproduced v0.60 stalled queue.
It covers focused construction/input/navigation journeys on the exact delivery
APK, rather than full matches, all Campaign missions or phone performance.
The emulator's existing dark-terrain rendering limitation remains.
