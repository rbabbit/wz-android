# v0.62 — Full-circle two-finger rotation

The engine already supported unlimited yaw. The v0.61 gesture classifier
incorrectly locked tilt when one finger stayed fixed and the other moved around
it: the midpoint moved before rotation was recognized. This was reproduced on
the actual native camera, which kept yaw at zero while changing pitch during
both clockwise and counterclockwise full-circle finger movements.

The shared touch adapter now distinguishes angular movement from coherent
parallel movement. A fixed or nearly fixed finger can act as a pivot; opposing
and unequal finger motion can turn the camera in either direction. Angle
accumulation crosses the circle boundary and continues through repeated turns.
The pending native-input budget stays bounded and is discarded on release; it
does not impose a camera angle limit. Native rotation remains rate based, so
finger and camera angles are not calibrated one to one.

Parallel two-finger vertical movement still tilts, radial movement still zooms,
and horizontal parallel movement still pans. Slow fractional tilt movement is
retained instead of being discarded. Accepted one-finger pan functions, world
input fences and lift/cancel/menu handling remain preserved. Rotating continues
to invalidate cached visible-truck selection.

The change applies to Towers, Campaign and FFA. Product changes are the shared
touch adapter and version only. All native JS/WASM/data, construction bridges,
missions, maps, other UI, saves, artwork, music, rewards and ad code are frozen
to the delivered v0.61 complete source. Its APK/full source are pinned through
`archive/fallback-0.62.json`.

Verification is recorded in `archive/rotation-0.62-host-checks.json`,
`archive/rotation-0.62-emulator-checks.json` and
`archive/rotation-clean-apk-0.62.json`. Host fixtures cover realistic pivot,
adjacent, opposing and unequal strokes plus the existing gesture lifecycle.
Native camera observations use ordinary prepared saves and starter units on
the exact delivery APK, with intermediate angles to distinguish a complete
turn from an unchanged endpoint. Focused input/navigation and compact layouts
are covered; no full-match, later-mission or phone-performance claim is made.
