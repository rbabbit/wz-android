# v0.63 — More consistent rotation recognition

Two native v0.62 failures were reproduced with ordinary, uninterrupted input:
a 20-degree pivot with fingers 40px apart did not turn the camera, and opening
the finger spacing slightly before a full turn locked zoom for the whole
gesture even though no zoom step was emitted. Ideal constant-radius circles
had missed these natural variations.

Rotation recognition uses smaller close-finger slop and signed angular
confidence. Clear sustained rotation can take over from an early zoom, tilt or
pan classification. The transition releases the previous native input and
rebases the gesture instead of replaying old movement. Pan-to-rotation waits
for the native mouse release/rearm barrier. Delayed recognition is cancelled
when the finger pair, session or menu changes.

Pure pinch, parallel tilt/pan, ordinary one-finger movement, full turns in both
directions and lift/cancel cleanup remain supported. Only the shared touch
adapter and application version change. All native programs/data, scenarios,
construction bridges, other UI, saves, artwork, music and ads remain frozen
to the delivered v0.62 complete source. Its APK/full source are pinned in
`archive/fallback-0.63.json`.

Focused host and exact-APK native checks cover short adjacent-finger turns,
mixed spacing/rotation, uneven strokes, deliberate handoff, ordinary gesture
controls, input restoration and compact screens. Prepared ordinary Easy saves
and starter units avoid unrelated construction or long-running bot fixtures.
Native observations are taken around uninterrupted strokes rather than
repeated held-gesture saves. These checks do not establish behavior on every
phone's touch hardware or full-match gameplay.
