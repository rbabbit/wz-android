# v0.66 — FFA maps and music

FFA now offers EMAG FFA, Contact, Ng-TemplarFFA, RB-Simple, RB_Usefull and
EMAJ-6P. Swipe the real terrain overview or use its arrow to choose a map; the
setup shows its name, dimensions, authored starting conditions and opponent
count. Difficulty remains independent of the map choice. Selection, saved-game
returns and replay retain the actual map identity.

The four-player maps use one human and three independent Cobra opponents.
EMAJ-6P uses one human and five. Native Easy/Medium/Hard and VTOL-off restrictions
apply to every opponent. The maps' authored data are retained: Contact starts
with sixteen trucks and no labs/factories; Templar keeps its legacy map format
and water; Simple, Usefull and EMAJ keep their own authored bases and neutral
features. Original archives, hashes, credits and licences are included in the
complete source. Previews are rendered from native terrain data, not generated
battle illustrations.

Contact's authored base contains thirteen or fourteen power generators per
player. Its native generator limit is raised to fourteen before map loading,
then kept at that floor after stock setup. This prevents the engine dropping
authored generators and leaving their modules without owners. Other maps keep
their existing limits; the original map files are not edited.

Launch, HUD and save/restore validation now use the selected map's native level,
slot count, bot identity and native AI contexts. Missing old EMAG metadata still
means its original four slots and original bot family; it is never silently
relabeled as a different map or AI. Native archives are checked before atomic
commit, including map/slot configuration, hostile alliances, unique bot
contexts and the native rules session. Existing saved ZIPs are not rewritten.

FFA and Campaign have the same compact MUSIC menu and remembered on/off setting
as Towers. All soundtrack bytes are already bundled. FFA uses a shuffled bag of
all 39 songs, advancing when a song actually ends. Campaign uses the three
original in-game soundtrack tracks: random selection at native mission/session
initialization, then ordered advance. Video playback retains and pauses the
current song; repeated HUD observations and briefing replay do not restart it.
Native mission results suspend this in-game playlist. The stock frontend/menu
cue is separate and is not claimed as reproduced. Towers keeps its assigned
map track and loop behavior. Sound effects are not controlled by MUSIC.

The original playlist rules and exact sources are documented in
[the music audit](../music-0.66-source-audit.md). The Guide's FFA and music pages
describe the new selection and playlist behavior.

Scope is FFA map packaging/runtime/setup/save identity, shared music controls,
Campaign music loading, and related Guide copy. Compiled native programs,
Towers/Campaign maps, stock Cobra logic, gestures, original music, ads and
rewards remain preserved. Delivered v0.65 APK and full source are retained by
`archive/fallback-0.66.json`.

Validation artifacts record focused host checks and the exact delivery-APK
emulator journey, including all six map starts, six-player and legacy saves,
media decoding/lifecycle and compact navigation. Synthetic protocol fixtures
are labelled separately from native engine observations. An emulator analyser
can establish decoded PCM and playback state; it does not establish audible
output from a physical phone speaker. Full campaign/AI balance playthroughs are
outside this change.
