# Optional Google Play Games preparation — 0.99.12

This revision prepares the app side of an optional Google Play Games integration. It does not activate a configured Google Play Games service. Play Console setup has been deferred by the user: the application ID and achievement/leaderboard mappings in `app/src/main/assets/warzone/play-games.json` remain blank.

The feature is OFF by default. The manifest removes the SDK's automatic initialization provider; the app initializes the SDK only after an explicit Connect action and valid configuration. Opening the app does not sign in or initialize Play Games. Local play, local saves and Crystals remain available without a Google connection. Google Play Games Services SDK dependency: `com.google.android.gms:play-services-games-v2:22.1.0`.

Before any real service use, the publisher must configure the correct application/package and signing-certificate credentials, Saved Games, achievements and leaderboards in Play Console, then supply the public Console IDs. Accounts using an unpublished Play Games project need Play Games tester access; app-track tester access alone does not complete that configuration. See Google's [Android setup guide](https://developer.android.com/games/pgs/android/android-start), [tester-access instructions](https://developer.android.com/games/pgs/console/publish#enable_accounts_for_testing) and [Play Games Services terms](https://developer.android.com/games/pgs/terms). Sign-in, cloud backup and submissions have not been activated against a configured project in this revision.

Cloud backup is a manual replacement operation with a 3 MiB compressed payload limit. Its supported scope includes the five native saved-game folders (Campaign, Towers, Survival, Challenge and FFA/Maps), the complete Crystals/progress/inventory/loadout ledger, and installed downloaded-map archives with their installation metadata. Account credentials, network data, device settings, previews and catalogue caches are excluded. The archive has a versioned app-identity manifest and SHA-256 checksums, with bounds of 512 files and 64 MiB expanded data. A backup exceeding the service payload limit fails clearly and keeps local data; files are never silently omitted to fit.

Restore requires user confirmation from Home. Files, wallet and required map assets are staged and validated before replacement. The prior device state is retained as a private `files/play-games-backups/before-restore-*.zip` recovery archive. A durable journal permits rollback after an interrupted restore. Wallet snapshots replace the saved ledger; they do not merge balances or add credits. The app reloads wallet/catalogue state after successful restore. Missing or incompatible map installations prevent the restore before local saves or wallet data are replaced.

No app tests, emulator tests, Google sign-in tests, achievement/leaderboard submissions or real cloud-service tests were performed. The app-side implementation remains unverified at runtime. No VPS update or GitHub/Google Play upload is part of this change.
