# Play Games leaderboard IDs 0.99.16

Version code: 116.

Three leaderboards now use the real Google Play Games IDs from the user's updated `games-ids (1).xml` export:

- Campaign — First Mission — Medium.
- FFA — EMAG — Easy — Cobra.
- Challenge — Firefight — Easy — Cannon.

Each ranks the fastest eligible victory in milliseconds with lower times first. Submission uses the matching mode, map, difficulty, bot, player count, team setup and strategy classification. These boards do not combine unrelated game setups.

All 18 achievement IDs from 0.99.15 remain linked. No Towers, Survival, Maps or Multiplayer leaderboard IDs were supplied, so those boards remain unconfigured. Play Games remains optional.

No tests, emulator checks, browser actions, QA, uploads or publishing were performed for this revision. Score submission and online leaderboard behavior remain unverified at runtime.
