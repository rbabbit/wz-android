# Play Games mint 0.99.21

Version code: 121.

The native Play Games page follows the selected first mint-and-gold mockup: a near-black charcoal base, mint account accents and controls, filled mint active tabs, and individual rounded achievement cards. Unlocked cards use dark green surfaces, gold stars and mint labels; locked icons use muted blue and lavender.

Refresh remains removed. Opening the page, changing tabs or leaderboard timeframes, and retrying failed requests continue to load Google data. Account details, achievement progress and leaderboard scores remain connected to the existing Google APIs.

Account guards, optional sign-in, all 18 achievements, three leaderboards, account history, cloud actions and saves are unchanged. The change is limited to the page's native styling and version documentation.

No tests, QA, emulator checks, browser actions or uploads were performed. The mockup's native interpretation has not been inspected at runtime.
